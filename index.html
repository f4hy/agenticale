      Recursive Self-Improvement
      
Every pint makes
the next pint better.

      
After each batch, our agents evaluate the beer, rewrite the recipe, and then rewrite the agent that rewrites the recipe. Then they rewrite that agent.

      
We expect a brewing intelligence explosion sometime in Q3, weather permitting. Fermentation is temperature-sensitive.

    Technology
    
The full stack.

    
For investors pattern-matching on keywords, here is every one of them.

    

      
Mixture of Yeasts MoE
A sparse gating network routes each batch to one of 64 expert yeast strains. Usually it's US-05.

      
RLHF alignment
Reinforcement Learning from Human Fermentation. Reward signal: someone at the party says "oh, that's actually good."

      
Chain-of-Hops reasoning
The model hops step by step. Each hop addition is justified in a hidden scratchpad nobody reads.

      
Test-time compute inference
The longer the agent thinks about the beer, the better the beer. We let it think for about six weeks. This is called lagering.

      
Context window 64 oz
Industry-leading growler-sized context. Long-context recall degrades after the third pour.

      
Tokenomics web3 (legacy)
Each pint is a token. Tokens are burned on consumption. Supply is strictly deflationary on Friday nights.

      
Malt Context Protocol MCP
An open standard that lets any agent talk to any fermenter. The fermenters have not adopted it.

      
Synthetic data pretraining
When we ran out of real tasting notes, the agents wrote more. They are now 94% "hints of stone fruit."

      
Distillation legal review pending
We distill a large brewing model into a smaller one. We do not distill the beer. That would require a different licence.

      
Vibe brewing DX
Describe the beer you want in natural language. The agents brew an IPA.

      
Human-in-the-loop safety
A human is kept in the loop at all times, primarily to lift heavy things.

      
Responsible Scaling Policy governance
We will not exceed 8% ABV until alignment is solved.
